Q2: Deciding on important variables

Best subset selection: compute least squares fit for all possible subsets of
predictors, and then choose the “best”

“best” in terms of some criteria (Mallow’s GC, AIC, BIC, adjusted R?, etc.)

impossible for large p: 2? models in total (~ one billion model when p = 40)

Forward selection: start with no variables in the model,
add variables one-by-one. Greedy approach.

Backward selection: start with a full model, remove variables
one-by-one. Does not work when p > n

Mixed selection: combination of forward and backward selection
