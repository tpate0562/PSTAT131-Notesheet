Why bother considering LDA?

Reason 1: coefficient estimates in logistic regression can be unstable

Consider this example:

Y= 0 if xX <50

1 if x>50

Perfectly separable!

> x <- seq(100)

> y <- cCrep(@, 50), rep(1, 50))

> fit <- glm(y~x, family = "binomial")

Warning messages:

1: glm.fit: algorithm did not converge

2: glm.fit: fitted probabilities numerically @ or 1 occurred

LDA does not suffer from this problem!
