(a) \emph{Univariate Pattern}\ixh{missingness pattern}: $Y_1$--$Y_3$ complete, only $Y_4$ has a missing block at the bottom. (b) \emph{Monotone Pattern}: $Y_1,Y_2$ complete, $Y_3$ missing at the bottom third, $Y_4$ missing at the bottom half (each later variable missing for a superset of the rows).
