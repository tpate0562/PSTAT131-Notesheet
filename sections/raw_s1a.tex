\slide{1}{18}{[How a child vs.\ a program learns ``dog'' (New Yorker)]}
Old program: thousands of if-then-else rules (ears, snout, hair, not a rat \ldots). Child: sees labeled dogs, errs (wolf = dog), is corrected. ML, like the child, uses a classified \ix{training set}\ixh{training data}{} (dog / not dog), extracts features\ixh{feature}, tests itself on thousands of labeled images, and builds its own recognizer.
